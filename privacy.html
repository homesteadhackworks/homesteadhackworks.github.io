Privacy Policy

Effective [launch date] · Developer: Homestead Hackworks

Hen House Hustle is a game about raising a flock of chickens. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game itself does not ask for your name, email or any account, and your flock is saved only on your own device.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress, such as your flock, chicken names, day count, upgrades and settings, in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it. You can also delete it from the game's Settings screen.

Advertising

The free version shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location (country or region),
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and how you interact with ads.

Google handles this information under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings under Google › Ads.

Purchases

Purchases such as the Premium Coop are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can unlock it, but we never see your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for or store your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • The game has no chat or way to contact other players.

Children

Hen House Hustle is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, for example online leaderboards, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change goes live.
